    Capabilities

    Everything you need for rigorous research

    From quick literature searches to publication-grade systematic reviews, Evidentia handles the entire research workflow.

    Multi-Source Search

    Parallel queries across PubMed, Semantic Scholar, OpenAlex, ArXiv, CrossRef, and more. Results are deduplicated and ranked.

    Systematic Reviews

    PRISMA 2020-compliant pipeline with identification, deduplication, multi-pass screening, quality scoring, and contradiction analysis.

    Academic Writing

    Built-in LaTeX editor with 10+ journal templates (IEEE, Nature, ACM, APA, etc.), plain-to-LaTeX conversion, and .tex export.

    Provenance Tracking

    Every claim is grounded in evidence with full citation chains, confidence scores, and reproducible fingerprints you can verify.

    Statistical Synthesis

    Automated meta-analysis with effect size extraction, forest plots, heterogeneity testing (I², Q), and fixed/random-effects models.

    Citation Export

    Export to BibTeX, RIS, APA, CSL-JSON, or directly to Zotero. Import existing libraries in .bib or .ris format.

    Workflow

    How it works

    Three modes for every stage of your research.

    01

    Quick Research

    Ask any research question. The AI agent decomposes it into sub-questions, searches academic databases, gathers evidence, and synthesizes verified claims with full citations.

    02

    Systematic Review

    Define inclusion/exclusion criteria and databases. Evidentia runs a PRISMA-compliant pipeline: identify, deduplicate, screen, score quality, detect contradictions, and export results.

    03

    Write & Export

    Draft in plain English and auto-convert to LaTeX, or work directly in LaTeX with journal templates. Export .tex files, BibTeX citations, and PRISMA flow diagrams.
